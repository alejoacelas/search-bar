# AI power users

This is a reusable discovery list, not a ranking of people. The seed was the top public contributors to ten active open-source coding-agent repositories. I kept maintainers with one of these signals:

- at least 500 commits in a seed repository;
- creator or core maintainer status plus substantial independent open-source work;
- at least 100 commits in a seed repository and a public project relevant to personal search, retrieval, mail, messages, or memory.

Commit counts depend on repository and merge history. They show sustained public work, not authorship quality or AI usage by themselves. I checked profiles and repositories before keeping an account. `roboomp` and `actions-user` were excluded as automation-like accounts; company-suffixed accounts with no reusable personal work were omitted.

[Download the CSV](ai-power-users.csv) for reuse.
